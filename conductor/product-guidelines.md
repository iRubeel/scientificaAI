### Tone and Voice
MathReferee should adopt a **Formal and Authoritative** tone and voice in all its communications and user interface elements. This approach emphasizes precision, objectivity, and academic rigor, which are crucial for a system acting as an AI referee in mathematical and statistical domains. A formal and authoritative tone will foster trust in the AI's judgments and recommendations, aligning with the serious and analytical nature of scientific peer review.

### Visual Identity (Inferred)
Given the formal and authoritative tone, the visual identity should be clean, professional, and minimalist. Focus on clarity and readability, using a palette that conveys seriousness and reliability. Avoid overly playful or informal design elements.
