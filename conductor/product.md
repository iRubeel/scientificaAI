# Product Overview: Revly

## Initial Concept
Revly is an autonomous AI referee system for mathematical and statistical papers using Gemini Pro.

## Target Users
Revly aims to serve:
*   **Researchers and academics** in mathematics and statistics who need efficient and reliable review of their papers or papers in their field.
*   **Students and educators** in STEM fields who can benefit from detailed, AI-driven analysis of mathematical and statistical content.
*   **Publishers and peer-review committees** looking to streamline and enhance the quality of their paper review processes.

## Core Functionalities
The system provides the following core capabilities:
*   **Automated Review**: Conducts autonomous review of mathematical and statistical papers.
*   **Academic Database Integration**: Seamlessly integrates with academic databases such as arXiv and Semantic Scholar for comprehensive literature search and context.
*   **Self-Critique and Epistemic Tracking**: Incorporates advanced AI mechanisms for self-critique of its own reasoning and epistemic tracking, which monitors confidence and uncertainty in claims.

## Key Differentiators
Revly distinguishes itself through:
*   **Fully Autonomous Review with Multi-step Reasoning**: Unlike traditional systems, Revly provides a complete, autonomous review process, utilizing multi-step reasoning to deeply analyze paper content.
*   **Integration of Specialized Tools**: Leverages a suite of specialized tools including arXiv for paper retrieval, Semantic Scholar for literature search, SymPy for symbolic math, and SciPy/statsmodels for statistical analysis.
*   **Epistemic Tracking and Multi-level Self-Critique**: Features robust epistemic tracking to assess the confidence and uncertainty of claims, combined with a multi-level self-critique mechanism to ensure accuracy and coherence of its findings.

## High-Level Goals & Objectives
The primary goals for Revly are:

*   **Enhance Reliability and Efficiency**: Significantly improve the reliability and efficiency of scientific paper review, reducing human workload and potential biases.
*   **Provide Comprehensive and Transparent Review**: Offer a thorough and transparent review process, making the AI's reasoning and findings understandable.
*   **Accelerate Scientific Discovery**: By streamlining the peer-review process, Revly aims to accelerate the pace of scientific discovery and knowledge dissemination.
