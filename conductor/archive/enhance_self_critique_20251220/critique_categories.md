# Enhanced Critique Categories and Criteria

## 1. Logical Consistency
*   **Logical Gap:** A step in the reasoning that does not follow from the previous steps.
*   **Circular Reasoning:** The conclusion is used as a premise.
*   **Contradiction:** Two statements or results that cannot both be true.
*   **Non-Sequitur:** A conclusion that is unrelated to the premises.

## 2. Assumption Validity
*   **Unstated Assumption:** A premise required for the argument but not explicitly stated.
*   **Strong Assumption:** An assumption that is too restrictive or unlikely to hold.
*   **Violated Assumption:** An assumption made but later contradicted by the analysis or data.

## 3. Methodological Soundness
*   **Inappropriate Method:** Using a technique or tool not suitable for the problem.
*   **Incorrect Implementation:** Correct method chosen but applied incorrectly (e.g., wrong parameters).
*   **Missing Baseline:** Failure to compare results against a standard benchmark.
*   **Overfitting:** Model or analysis tailored too closely to specific data.

## 4. Evidence Sufficiency
*   **Insufficient Data:** Drawing conclusions from a sample size too small for statistical significance.
*   **Biased Sample:** Data used does not represent the population.
*   **Weak Evidence:** Supporting evidence is tangential or low quality.

## 5. Statistical Rigor
*   **p-hacking:** manipulating data or analysis until non-significant results become significant.
*   **Misinterpretation of p-values:** Confusing statistical significance with practical significance.
*   **Violated Statistical Assumptions:** e.g., assuming normality when data is skewed.

## 6. Clarity & Notation
*   **Ambiguous Notation:** Symbols used without definition or inconsistently.
*   **Vague Definitions:** Terms used without clear, precise meaning.
*   **Structure Issues:** Poor organization making the argument hard to follow.
